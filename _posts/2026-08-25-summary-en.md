## [Apple unveils M6 2nm chip and M5 Ultra quad-die flagship](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 25, 2026, Apple announced the M6 chip, its first 2nm processor with a 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, and Dual 16-core Neural Engine, alongside the M5 Ultra, the company&\#x27;s most powerful chip ever, featuring a first-of-its-kind quad-die architecture built with next-generation UltraFusion technology. The M6 and M5 Ultra represent a major leap in on-device AI compute and performance for Apple&\#x27;s Mac lineup. They intensify competition in the PC and workstation market, while enabling more demanding AI workloads directly on Macs. The M6 is fabricated on a 2nm process and features a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, which Apple says doubles AI-related performance compared to previous Apple silicon. The M5 Ultra pairs two M5 Max dual-die chips via UltraFusion to create a quad-die design, a first for the M-series.

The chip&\#x27;s Neural Engine is dedicated to on-device machine learning and AI tasks, while advances in process nodes like 2nm enable higher performance and efficiency.

## [OpenAI&\#x27;s Jalapeño Chip Claims to Beat Nvidia Blackwell in Inference](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI and Broadcom&\#x27;s custom inference chip, named Jalapeño, reportedly outperforms Nvidia&\#x27;s Blackwell GPUs in inference benchmarks. On SemiAnalysis&\#x27; InferenceX benchmark, it delivered more tokens per user and higher throughput per kilowatt than current state-of-the-art inference processors. This development signals a potential shift in the AI hardware landscape, directly challenging Nvidia&\#x27;s dominance in AI inference chips. If independently verified, it could lower inference costs, improve energy efficiency, and affect the economics of large-scale AI deployment for cloud providers and enterprises. Jalapeño is a custom AI inference chip announced on June 24, 2026, with first benchmark results released on August 25, 2026. The results come from SemiAnalysis&\#x27; InferenceX benchmark, but the claims have not yet been independently verified by third parties.

Custom inference chips like Jalapeño are designed specifically for large language model inference, prioritizing efficiency and throughput over general-purpose compute. OpenAI has been increasingly investing in custom silicon to reduce its reliance on Nvidia&\#x27;s GPUs for serving AI models.

## [Nitter Cease-and-Desist Forces All Instances Offline](https://github.com/zedeus/nitter/issues/1442) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Nitter has received cease and desist letters, and all Nitter instances are now down for the foreseeable future. The project maintainer announced this in GitHub issue \#1442, saying they are awaiting legal advice. This is significant because Nitter is a widely-used privacy-preserving front-end for Twitter/X, and its shutdown highlights the legal fragility of open-source scraping and alternative interfaces. It could push more users toward corporate-controlled platforms and consolidate X&\#x27;s control over access to its content. The maintainer says they have received cease and desist letters and are awaiting legal advice, with all instances down until further notice. The letters&\#x27; origin has not been publicly detailed, but the timing has led some to speculate it is linked to AI companies using Nitter/xcancel to access tweets.

hackernews · Banditoz · Aug 25, 17:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437283)

**Background**: Nitter is a free and open-source alternative frontend for Twitter/X that lets people browse tweets without JavaScript, tracking, ads, or an account. It was designed to be lighter and faster than Twitter, but it relies on scraping Twitter&\#x27;s unofficial endpoints, which makes it legally fragile. Cease and desist letters are a common tool for companies to pressure projects that use their services or content without authorization.
