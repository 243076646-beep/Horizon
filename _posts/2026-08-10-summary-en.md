## [Meta&\#x27;s Muse Glimmer: 30B open-weight model for always-on local agents](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Meta has introduced Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weight model \(Apache 2.0\) optimized for always-on local agent workflows, capable of running on a single consumer GPU. The company also announced that open weights for its Muse Spark 1.2 foundation model will be released soon. This release pushes agentic AI toward local, private, and low-latency execution, reducing reliance on cloud infrastructure and enabling new always-on assistant use cases. It also strengthens Meta&\#x27;s position in the open-weights AI landscape, especially against Chinese open-weight models and proprietary frontier rivals. Muse Glimmer integrates multi-step reasoning, reliable tool use, multimodal understanding, and failure recovery into a single model that runs entirely locally without cloud infrastructure or network access. Meta reports performance of up to 20K tokens per second on edge and workstation NVIDIA platforms, and the model is also available on Hugging Face for direct download.

**Background**: Always-on local agent workflows are AI systems that continuously run on a device, processing local data and executing complex tasks such as function calling, coding, and evaluation without sending data to the cloud. Dense models around 30 billion parameters are small enough to fit on consumer GPUs while retaining strong reasoning and agentic capabilities. Meta&\#x27;s Muse series includes the proprietary Muse Spark foundation model; by releasing open weights for both Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark, Meta aims to drive ecosystem adoption and compete in the open-weights AI market.

## [EU PPWR/EPR Rules Hit Cross-Border Sellers with New Registration Fees](https://www.reddit.com/r/ecommerce/comments/1vketgs/important_info_about_ppwr_epr_regulations_in_the/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

As of 12 August, any company selling in another EU country must register as a packaging producer in that country and appoint a local representative. Registration costs range from 200 to 900 EUR per country per year, potentially totaling close to 20,000 EUR for all EU markets. This regulatory shift creates a heavy administrative and financial burden for micro and small e-commerce businesses, possibly ending their cross-border operations in the EU. Consumers in smaller EU markets may soon see niche and specialty products disappear from online stores and marketplaces. Each EU country has its own registration procedures, and the quoted price depends on the EPR compliance service used. The European Commission is accepting feedback on the packaging producer register rules until 10 September, and a Change.org petition opposing the cross-border EPR fees has gathered over 18,000 signatures.

reddit · r/ecommerce · /u/Melodic-Piccolo5751 · Aug 10, 08:15

**Background**: PPWR is the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation \(Regulation \(EU\) 2025/40\), which replaces the earlier Packaging Waste Directive. It requires all packaging on the EU market to be recyclable in an economically viable way by 2030. Extended Producer Responsibility \(EPR\) laws hold packaging producers accountable for the costs of managing packaging waste, often through contributions to a Producer Responsibility Organization. Many EU countries now require sellers to register in their national producer registers and appoint local representatives.
