## [Number-Named AI Labs Proliferate: ElevenLabs, TwelveLabs and More](https://quantumi.sh/public/labs.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A whimsical community discussion highlights the growing trend of AI companies named after numbers, such as ElevenLabs, TwelveLabs, and ThirteenLabs, even coining jokes about future labs like 47 Labs. The discussion also surfaces real examples like 41labs.ai. This naming trend reflects the AI industry&\#x27;s crowded and often confusing branding landscape, where startups try to stand out with memorable, numeric identities. It also shows how the tech community enjoys spotting and riffing on these naming patterns. Commenters note that some labs may be named after street addresses, and one mentions registering domain names like sixsevenlabs is already taken. TwelveLabs and ElevenLabs have even co-hosted a &\#x27;23Labs Hackathon&\#x27;, while ThirteenLabs originated as a hackathon project that turns videos into 3D experiences.

hackernews · jemoka · Aug 22, 14:54 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49400408)

**Background**: ElevenLabs is a well-known AI voice synthesis company founded in 2022 by former Google engineer Piotr Dąbkowski and Mateusz Staniszewski. TwelveLabs is a video understanding platform whose models are now available on Amazon Bedrock. The discussion is part of a broader trend of AI startups adopting numeric brand names.
