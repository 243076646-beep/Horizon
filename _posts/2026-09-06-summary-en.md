## [Private German rocket makes history by reaching orbit from European soil](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/isar-aerospace-second-launch-norway-andoya-spaceport-spectrum-rocket) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Isar Aerospace&\#x27;s Spectrum rocket reached orbit on a second test flight from Andøya Spaceport in Norway, becoming the first privately developed European orbital launch vehicle to do so from European soil. The company had lost its first Spectrum test vehicle in March 2025 when it crashed shortly after liftoff. This milestone shows that private European launch providers can develop orbital capability, strengthening Europe&\#x27;s strategic autonomy in space. It could encourage more investment in European space startups and reduce Europe&\#x27;s reliance on non-European launch infrastructure. Spectrum is a two-stage orbital launch vehicle designed for small and medium satellites. Isar Aerospace has arranged launches from both Andøya Spaceport in Norway and the Guiana Space Centre in French Guiana, giving the rocket flexible options for different missions.

hackernews · bookmtn · Sep 5, 20:31 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580369)

**Background**: Isar Aerospace is a German aerospace startup founded in 2018 near Munich and named after the river that flows through the city. Its Spectrum rocket has been under development for years as a privately funded European alternative for reaching orbit. Many European launchers historically fly from the overseas Guiana Space Centre, so launching from Andøya in Norway is a significant step for launching from within Europe itself.
