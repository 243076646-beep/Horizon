## [TheNumbers.com Shuts Down After Scraping Attacks](https://stephenfollows.com/p/what-just-happened-to-thenumberscom-should-worry-us-all) ⭐️ 8.0/10

TheNumbers.com, a popular movie box office data site, was taken offline due to aggressive web scraping and malicious attacks, then relaunched with limited data. This incident highlights the vulnerability of data-driven websites to automated scraping and attacks, potentially discouraging the operation of free data resources. It also raises concerns about prediction markets using scraped data for unfair advantage. The site went down after being hammered by bots and malicious users seeking privileged access for prediction market betting. It returned with a fraction of its original data and a reduced design.

TheNumbers.com was a key source for movie box office data, valuable for prediction markets and industry analysis.

## [Startups Urge US Not to Ban Chinese Open-Weight AI](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/07/22/startup-founders-urge-trump-not-to-shut-off-chinese-open-weight-ai-01008992) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A group of startup founders has sent a letter to the U.S. government, urging it not to prohibit Chinese open-weight AI models, arguing that such a ban would harm American innovation and be difficult to enforce. This debate highlights a critical tension between national security concerns and the open-source AI ecosystem, with significant implications for global AI development, regulatory policy, and the competitiveness of U.S. startups. The letter specifically addresses open-weight models, which include publicly released trained parameters, making them easily downloadable and usable even if the training data or methods remain proprietary.

hackernews · theanonymousone · Jul 23, 15:18 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49023016)

**Background**: Open-weight AI models are models whose final trained parameters \(weights and biases\) are publicly released, allowing anyone to download, run, and fine-tune them on their own hardware. This contrasts with fully closed models where only API access is provided. The U.S. government has considered restricting Chinese open-weight models due to concerns about intellectual property theft and misuse by adversaries.

## [Building Email Infrastructure from Scratch Achieves 47.7% Open Rate](https://www.reddit.com/r/ecommerce/comments/1v4muev/went_from_zero_email_infrastructure_to_a_working/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A marketer spent six weeks warming up a domain and built a branching welcome flow that achieved a 47.7% open rate on the first email, significantly above the industry average of 30%. This case study demonstrates that proper email infrastructure setup and behavioral segmentation can dramatically improve ecommerce email engagement, offering a replicable strategy for stores with no existing email systems. The welcome flow branched on Day 2 based on whether the recipient opened Email 1, sending a faster path to engaged users and a re-engagement angle to non-openers; the engaged branch maintained over 50% opens through Email 4.

reddit · r/ecommerce · /u/gist-of-everything · Jul 23, 18:47

**Background**: Email deliverability depends on sender reputation, which is built via authentication protocols like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and a domain warm-up period where sending volume gradually increases. Without these, emails often land in spam. Many ecommerce brands lack proper email infrastructure, missing conversion opportunities from signups that don&\#x27;t immediately buy.
