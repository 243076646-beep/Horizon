## [119 MEPs Push EU Commission to Enforce AI &\#x27;Nudifier&\#x27; Ban from Day One](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiZ0FVX3lxTE02QlZROWtXNHd5YlhJT2VZSTZmX0JHSUpOenJYTUo1d0I3bzNHTTZLUFpBcFdtVTdtTk11QlYwbkVVZDA2eS1fVjJwWDdQNWtja1BuNkxiWUFDNndLUUZWVHZ5VnY0S0U?oc=5) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A group of 119 Members of the European Parliament is pressing the European Commission and national governments to guarantee that the EU&\#x27;s newly agreed ban on AI &\#x27;nudifier&\#x27; systems can be enforced from the very first day it takes effect. The ban targets AI systems that generate child sexual abuse material or create sexualized images, video and audio of an identifiable person without their consent. It highlights the gap between passing a prohibition and actually being able to enforce it: without clear enforcement mechanisms, penalties and platform cooperation, the ban could remain symbolic. The outcome will shape how strictly the EU AI Act is applied to generative AI abuse, affecting AI developers, hosting platforms and victims of non-consensual intimate imagery. The prohibition is part of the AI Act package that EU co-legislators agreed to in 2026 alongside simplification measures, and it explicitly covers AI systems producing child sexual abuse material or non-consensual sexual depictions of identifiable people. Its practical bite depends on the Act&\#x27;s staggered applicability dates, with high-risk Annex III obligations under the AI Act applying from August 2, 2026.

**Background**: The EU AI Act \(Regulation 2024/1689\) is the world&\#x27;s first comprehensive AI regulation, laying down a uniform, risk-based legal framework for developing and using AI in the EU. &\#x27;Nudifier&\#x27; systems are generative AI tools that digitally remove clothing from photos to create fake nude or sexually explicit images, often of real people without consent. Because such tools are commercially widespread online, lawmakers added an explicit ban on them during the 2026 revision, and MEPs now want reassurance that it will be enforced immediately rather than after a delay.
