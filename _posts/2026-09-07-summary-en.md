## [Asahi Linux Announces Official Support for Apple M3 Macs](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Asahi Linux has announced official support for Apple M3 Macs, a major milestone for running Linux on Apple Silicon. This support was achieved through extensive reverse-engineering of the M3 hardware to bring up the Linux kernel and device drivers. This expands the Linux ecosystem to Apple&\#x27;s latest Mac hardware, giving users a viable open-source alternative to macOS. It also highlights how community reverse-engineering can keep pace with Apple&\#x27;s proprietary silicon platforms. Some features remain incomplete, such as sleep/wake and HDMI support, which could hinder adoption. Early user reports also show GPU compute performance \(e.g., llama.cpp\) still trails the native Metal backend on macOS.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a volunteer-driven project that ports the Linux kernel and related software to Apple Silicon Macs. Because Apple provides no public documentation for its M-series chips, the project reverse-engineers the hardware and driver interfaces. The effort started with the M1 chip and has gradually expanded to newer SoCs like M2 and now M3.

**Background**: The EU AI Act is the bloc&\#x27;s landmark risk-based law for artificial intelligence, while the Cyber Resilience Act sets cybersecurity requirements for digital products and services. Together they form the so-called &\#x27;three layers&\#x27;, but because each was drafted independently, they do not interoperate or share a common compliance framework.
